---
title: "Pause, redirect, and recover"
description: "Control active research and resume safely from durable checkpoints."
---

Opportunity Scout stores stage checkpoints so long research runs can be interrupted without losing
completed work.

## Controls

### Pause

Interrupt the active research subprocess and retain completed checkpoints. Resume later with a fresh
configured time window.

### Stop

End the active work while preserving the evidence and checkpoints already produced.

### Redirect

Add a new brief revision and choose whether to refine sources, redo market evidence, reframe from
product synthesis, or restart from customer discovery. Auto mode infers the intended impact.

### Recover after restart

Jobs that were queued or running become paused when the server restarts. Open the run and resume it
from the workspace.

Results from an obsolete brief revision cannot publish. Partial assessments remain visibly labeled
when timeouts, rate limits, or invalid output prevent a completed skeptical review.
