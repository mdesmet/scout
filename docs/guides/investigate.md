---
title: "Investigate a weak score"
description: "Run focused follow-up research without altering the original report."
---

Use a focused investigation when a score is Unknown or supported weakly and one research question
could materially improve the decision.

1. **Choose a scorecard.** Open a completed live report and select an opportunity. Choose
   **Investigate this unknown** or **Investigate further** on one dimension.
2. **Review the question.** Keep the suggested research question or refine it. Guidance applies to
   this investigation only; it does not become a global preference.
3. **Set a time limit and start.** The follow-up performs targeted evidence research followed by
   skeptical verification.
4. **Compare the result.** Review the previous and updated score, confidence, new observations,
   status change, and link back to the original report.

Finding nothing is a valid result: the score can remain Unknown. The original report, its other eight
scores, and its source records remain intact.

## Willingness-to-pay investigations

These specifically seek firsthand paid workarounds or buying intent. Vendor prices and operating
costs alone cannot establish a positive willingness-to-pay score.
