description: "Understand scorecards, Unknown values, confidence, and deterministic gates."

Opportunity Scout scores nine dimensions independently from 1 to 5. Higher is more favorable.

| Dimension | What it asks |
| --- | --- |
| Pain severity | How costly or disruptive is the problem? |
| Pain frequency | How often does it occur? |
| Willingness to pay | Is there firsthand spending or credible buying intent? |
| Demand momentum | Is the need growing or becoming more urgent? |
| Competitive opening | Is there a sourced reason to switch from alternatives? |
| Customer reachability | Can a solo founder reach this segment? |
| Solo feasibility | Can one founder build and operate a bounded first product? |
| Economic potential | Can the opportunity support a viable business? |
| Defensibility | Can an advantage compound over time? |

Unsupported dimensions remain **Unknown**. Unknown is not zero, and it is never filled in merely to
produce a complete-looking scorecard. Confidence and founder fit are separate from scores. There is
no composite score or probability of success.

## Minimum qualification shape

A recommendation needs corroborated firsthand pain, a sourced consequence or workaround, usable
support for the required score dimensions, no unresolved solo-founder blocker, and a coherent product
thesis. It also needs sourced alternatives, checked pricing, buying evidence, a reachable segment,
and a reason customers would switch.

::: warning
Qualification is a coverage and consistency check. It is not proof that customers will buy.
:::
